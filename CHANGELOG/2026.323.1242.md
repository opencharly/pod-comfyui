# 2026.323.1242 — feat(comfyui): carry the dynamic-vram flag the measurement used

## Summary

Head `daed860b3b046373fb784c5ea73fabccd496e542` — 2 files, +93 −9.

The branch carries two commits. `f73d4a2479af9b458a5c9ecd0663d2daf70c819b` restored
`--enable-dynamic-vram` to the ComfyUI launch line in `charly.yml` and argued, from ComfyUI
v0.45.3's own source, that the two flags are orthogonal rather than one mechanism under two
names: `--disable-smart-memory` is the prompt-boundary offload policy (`execution.py` calls
`unload_all_models()` on the success path of every prompt, and `free_memory()` asks for `1e32`
bytes instead of `memory_required - get_free_memory(device)`), while `--enable-dynamic-vram` is
the separate DynamicVRAM gate (`enables_dynamic_vram()` / `dynamic_vram_supported()` →
`comfy_aimdo.control.init()` and per-device `init_devices(..., vram_headroom)`). R2 permits
carrying both.

This head answers the two BLOCKs on that commit, and the bed the last verdict asked for has now
been run against it.

repo and has no bed of its own. The bed that owns this candy is `check-comfyui-pod` in
