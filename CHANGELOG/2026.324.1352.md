## Summary

Advances ComfyUI from **v0.45.3 to v0.46.1**, with the pixi pins following the release's own
`requirements.txt`.

- `charly.yml` — `COMFYUI_REF: v0.46.1`, plus two in-tree build-scope witnesses:
  `comfyui-pinned-ref-exact` (`/opt/comfyui/.charly-ref` is exactly `v0.46.1`) and
  `comfyui-pinned-packages` (the pixi env carries frontend `1.59.6`, templates `0.11.112`, docs
  `0.5.17`). Both literals are deliberate: a braced variable makes an assert-only step SKIP.
- `pixi.toml` / `pixi.lock` — `comfyui-frontend-package 1.59.6`,
  `comfyui-workflow-templates 0.11.112` (+ its `-core`/`-json`/`-media-assets` chain),
  `comfyui-embedded-docs 0.5.17`; `comfy-aimdo`/`comfy-kitchen` unchanged.
- No launch or feature change: the out-of-box launch line (`--disable-smart-memory`,
  `--vram-headroom`) and the Z-Image-Turbo `COMFYUI_MODEL_SET` default are untouched.
